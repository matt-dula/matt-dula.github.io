Matt received the B.S. degree in electrical and computer engineering from Baylor University, Waco, Texas, in 2023. His current research interests include distributed sensing and communications systems, RF signal processing, algorithms for distributed computing and coordination, and software-defined radios. He was a recipient of the 2026 IEEE AP-S Graduate Fellowship and received an Honorable Mention in the Student Paper Competition at the 2026 IEEE International Symposium on Antennas and Propagation. He was a Finalist in the Student Paper Competition at the 2024 IEEE Wireless and Microwave Technology Conference.
